HOW TO PLAY

Wax Pack Solitaire

  1. Start with 10 packs. Each pack has 15 different card numbers.
  2. The first time a number appears, it can help form a run. Later copies become duplicates.
  3. Trade 10 duplicates for 1 pack or 25 duplicates for 3 packs.
  4. Any five consecutive unused numbers earn a bonus pack automatically. Those five numbers are then consumed for run purposes.
  5. Find #482 Rickey Henderson to win. You lose when no packs or affordable trades remain.
